Title: NIST AI 800-1 (2pd) — Managing Misuse Risk for Dual-Use Foundation Models
Authors: U.S. AI Safety Institute (NIST)
Venue: NIST publication, Second Public Draft (the file downloaded is the 2pd version; original instructions referenced ipd2)
Link: https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.800-1.ipd2.pdf  (resolves to the current NIST.AI.800-1 draft via redirect; DOI 10.6028/NIST.AI.800-1.2pd)
GitHub: (none)

Summary:
NIST AI 800-1 is the U.S. AI Safety Institute's guidance document for developers of dual-use foundation models, fulfilling tasking under EO 14110 / subsequent AI executive actions. It defines a misuse-risk lifecycle — anticipate, assess, manage, and report — and lays out seven concrete objectives covering threat-profile development, evaluation prior to release, mitigation deployment, transparency to government and downstream actors, and post-release monitoring. For each objective the draft enumerates "practices" a developer should be able to evidence (e.g., documented threat models, red-team coverage records, decision criteria for go/no-go, mitigation efficacy measurement). The document is process-oriented and intentionally non-prescriptive about thresholds, leaving capability/risk cutoffs for industry and government negotiation. Defenders use it as a checklist and audit baseline: it maps internal safety work onto language regulators will recognize, and it scaffolds the kind of pre- and post-deployment evidence dossier that grant programs, procurement vehicles, and downstream attestations are starting to expect.
