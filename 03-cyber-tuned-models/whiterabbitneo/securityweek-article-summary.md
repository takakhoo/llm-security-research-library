## Article Overview

WhiteRabbitNeo version 2.5 is an uncensored generative AI tool designed for offensive security testing. Available on Hugging Face, it identifies vulnerabilities and generates exploit code while providing remediation guidance. The piece frames WhiteRabbitNeo as the open-weights, dual-use answer to the closed, safety-tuned commercial models — explicitly modeled as "an open source AI version of Metasploit."

## Key Quotes

**Andy Manoske, VP of Product at Kindo (the company shipping WhiteRabbitNeo):**
- "WhiteRabbitNeo is an offensive security gen-AI model... to achieve this, it must be uncensored."
- "There is a balance in creating a dual use tool... we tried to walk that balance by thinking of it as an open source AI version of Metasploit."
- "WhiteRabbitNeo attempts to make its exploit code easily recognizable by security controls by being difficult to obfuscate."
- On safety-tuned models: "It also means you can't ask security questions about your own infrastructure."

**Jason Soroko (Sectigo):** "WhiteRabbitNeo's new release is an important asset for offensive cybersecurity simulating adversarial tactics."

**Amit Zimerman (Oasis Security):** "The power of AI in offensive operations could be misused, potentially leading to ethical dilemmas if AI-driven tools fall into the wrong hands."

## Core Capabilities (per the article)

- Leverages MITRE vulnerability databases and threat intelligence sources.
- Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) for continuous updates on emerging CVEs and infrastructure configurations.
- Distributed under permissive licensing on Hugging Face (Llama derivatives + Qwen-2.5-Coder derivative).
- Pitched at red teamers, pentesters, and defenders who need to ask offensive questions about their own environments.
