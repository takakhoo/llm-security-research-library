SUMMARY (offensive-security LLM ecosystem role)
  Falade's paper is the most-cited single-author survey of the underground "evil GPT"
  phenomenon. Using a blog-mining methodology, it catalogs how ChatGPT, FraudGPT, and
  WormGPT are advertised, sold, and operationalized on cybercrime forums (Hack Forums, dark-
  web markets, Telegram channels) for phishing, BEC, malware authoring, and social
  engineering. Within the offensive-security LLM ecosystem this is the primary open-access
  reference that maps the *threat-actor* side of the market — what models are for sale, what
  they cost (subscription pricing), what capabilities are claimed vs. delivered, and how
  attackers chain them with traditional toolkits. Defensive-side papers (Ferrara 2024, Renaud
  et al., the BIS GenAI report) cite Falade as the canonical inventory of black-market LLM
  offerings circa 2023. The paper is also useful as a primary-source exhibit when arguing
  about jailbreak persistence and the supply-chain economics of uncensored fine-tunes
  (FraudGPT/WormGPT being early commercial repackagings of jailbroken or open Llama-derived
  models).
