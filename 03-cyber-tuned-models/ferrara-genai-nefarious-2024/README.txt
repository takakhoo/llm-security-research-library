SUMMARY (offensive-security LLM ecosystem role)
  Ferrara's "GenAI Against Humanity" is the most widely-cited academic taxonomy of nefarious
  GenAI use cases. It surveys the threat surface across deepfakes, AI-driven disinformation
  and elections interference, automated phishing and BEC, malware generation, jailbroken /
  uncensored-LLM marketplaces (WormGPT, FraudGPT, FreedomGPT, PoisonGPT), data-poisoning
  attacks against models, and AI-enabled fraud at scale. Within the offensive-security LLM
  ecosystem it serves as the academic-grade umbrella reference: where Falade 2023 is the
  primary-source inventory of black-market models, Ferrara is the conceptual framing —
  threat models, harm taxonomies, stakeholder analysis, and policy-mitigation menu — that
  later papers cite when motivating their work. It is particularly useful for situating any
  cyber-tuned or uncensored model (DarkBERT, WhiteRabbitNeo, Hartford's Dolphin, Labonne's
  abliterated models) within a structured taxonomy of dual-use risk, and for finding
  pointers to the specific deepfake-, malware-, and influence-operation sub-literatures.
